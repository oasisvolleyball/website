Where can I play volleyball in Abu Dhabi?

Join our mixed social games: Mondays 8–10 pm at ICS Khalidiya and Fridays 9–11 pm at Raha International School, Khalifa City. It's 35 AED a game for men and women aged 16+.

Can I play volleyball in Abu Dhabi as a beginner?

Yes. Complete beginners are welcome at our social games. Teams are balanced on the night, so everyone gets a good game.

Can I come on my own?

Yes, most people do. Sign up on your own and you'll be put into a team on the night. You can also bring friends.

How do I sign up for a game?

Sign up and pay in our app at least 24 hours before the game: volleyball-signup.vercel.app. If you don't pay in time, you move to the waitlist.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel at least 24 hours before the game. If you cancel later, please still pay or find someone to take your spot.

What should I bring?

Clean indoor sports shoes, sportswear and water. Knee pads are optional. Bring your Emirates ID for Raha International School (digital is fine).
